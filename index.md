### Current interests

My research focuses on quantum networks, exploring how to design them, evaluate their performance, and use them in practical settings. My main research interests include:

- Quantum network architectures and communication protocols
- Applications and performance benchmarking for the Quantum Internet
- Quantum key distribution (QKD): pilot deployments and practical applications

Across these areas, I aim to connect advances in quantum communication with the requirements of real-world networks, helping translate emerging technologies into useful services.

[previous research interests included serverless edge computing, IoT and M2M communications, architectures and protocols for local and mobile wireless networks]
